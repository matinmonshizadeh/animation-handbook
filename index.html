Animation Atlas

A visual reference of web animation techniques. Each entry is a self-contained demo with an explanation of the mechanic — open an HTML file, it runs offline.

01 — Scroll-Based

  • Parallax Depth-of-Field
    Five mountain layers with parallax translation and blur-based depth-of-field driven by scroll progress.
  • Parallax Scrolling
    Four SVG layers at different scroll speeds create a 3D depth illusion; adjustable speed multipliers show how ratios make or break the effect.
  • Reverse-Scrolling Columns
    A center column scrolls normally while two flanking columns scroll in reverse, looping infinitely — opposing motion creates strong directional contrast.
  • Cover Card to Fixed Header
    A hero cover card scrubs into a compact fixed header on scroll — height, font size, opacity, and blur all driven by a single eased progress value.
  • Fly-in Fly-out Contact List
    Contact rows translate and fade through entry, active, and exit zones as you scroll — a continuous bidirectional effect scrubbed to scroll position.
  • Stacking Cards
    Cards fan into a deck as they stack — per-index sticky offset creates the peek tab, CSS Scroll-Driven Animations drive the scale.
  • ScrollTrigger Animation
    The onEnter / onLeave lifecycle demonstrated with vanilla JS — four zones showing fade-on-enter, scrub, pin+scrub, and snap.
  • Scrub Animation
    A camera lens disassembles in five stages as you scroll — every transform welded to scroll position, no autoplay, perfectly reversible.
  • Pin Animation
    A phone mockup pins to the viewport while four features swap in sequence — CSS position: sticky, no JavaScript pinning.
  • Snap Scrolling
    CSS scroll-snap-type creates a paginated, magnetic feel — switch between mandatory, proximity, and none to feel the difference.
  • Scrollytelling
    Fractional chapter progress drives continuous depth crossfades across six ocean-layer chapters — the porthole visual responds between discrete states.
  • Reveal on Scroll
    Seven reveal techniques (fade, slide, scale, blur, clip-path mask, stagger) fired by IntersectionObserver at a configurable trigger line.
  • Stagger Reveal
    Groups reveal sequentially with cascading delays — forward, reverse, center-out, and random directions across a grid, list, and chip set.
  • Horizontal Scroll
    Vertical scroll translates a horizontal panel strip — a tall pinned section provides the scroll budget, translateX does the work.
  • Sticky Section
    The whole section pins to the viewport while its interior morphs through four states scrubbed to scroll position within the section.
  • Counter Animation
    Numbers count 0 → target with easing when the stats section enters the internal viewport — four formats, configurable easing, stagger support.
  • Progress Bar
    Reading progress indicator in three styles: top bar, circular ring, and side rail — all driven by scrollTop / (scrollHeight − clientHeight).
  • Section Wipe
    The next section slides over the current one via sticky z-index stacking — a scale-down on the receding layer adds depth to the transition.
  • Zoom Into Image
    clip-path expands from a framed card to full-bleed as you scroll — the portal effect is inset() collapsing from center outward.
