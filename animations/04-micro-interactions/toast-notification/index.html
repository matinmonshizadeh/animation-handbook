Toast Notification

Transient cards slide into a corner, stack, count down a progress bar, then auto-dismiss as the rest reflow to close the gap.

Trigger a toast →

Each toast auto-dismisses when its progress bar empties. Hover (or drag) to pause the countdown; tap, swipe, or press × to dismiss early.
