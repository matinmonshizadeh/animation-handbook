Heart / Like Burst

Tap to like — the heart pops and fills as a spray of small hearts bursts outward and fades, Twitter-style.

Likes 128

The burst is a short-lived particle system on a canvas overlay. Each tap spawns N particles with random angle/velocity; gravity and fade retire them within ~700ms. Un-liking is silent.
