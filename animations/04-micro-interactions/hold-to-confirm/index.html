Copy the prompt

Paste it into an AI assistant such as ChatGPT, Claude or Cursor to build this in your own project.

Add hold to confirm to [the button for a risky action, such as Delete]. While the visitor holds it down, a fill grows steadily, and the action runs only once it is full, when a tick shows. If they let go early, the fill drains back, nothing happens and a short hint says to keep holding; when the settings ask for it, the button also shakes. Holding Space or Enter works the same way, and on touch a long press must not open the browser's menu. Grow the fill with a transform by elapsed time, and announce the result to screen readers. If the visitor has reduced motion turned on, fill in a few steps without the shake. Match the settings listed below.
