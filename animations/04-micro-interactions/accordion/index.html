Accordion Open/Close

Each question opens smoothly to show its answer. Best for FAQ pages.

1

Click it

Press a question to open its answer and again to close it, or press Show me.

A stack of headings that each open to show more below them. It keeps a long page short and easy to scan.

When an answer grows into place, the questions below slide down smoothly instead of jumping, so you keep your place.

It depends on the page. Often opening one closes the others, which keeps the list short, while settings pages usually let several stay open.

For questions, settings or long sections that people read one at a time. If everything must be seen at once, show it all instead.

Avoid putting accordions inside accordions; two levels is the most people can follow.

Yes. Each question is a button, so Tab reaches it and Enter or Space opens it, and screen readers hear whether it is open.

Show only the headings at first; when one is pressed, its content box grows from nothing to its full height and the headings below slide down, and pressing it again shrinks it back. Animate the box to the content's real height, never to a large fixed maximum, or the timing looks wrong. When the settings include it, turn a small arrow to show which items are open.
