Cursor Follower

Custom element follows mouse with lerp lag — mix-blend-mode: difference inverts any background.

The easing factor controls lag: follower.x += (mouse.x - follower.x) * ease. At 0.15, about 15% of the remaining gap closes each frame — giving smooth, weighted following.
