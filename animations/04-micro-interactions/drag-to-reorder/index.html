Drag to Reorder

Drag an item and the others slide aside to make room. Best for to-do lists.

1

Drag it

Drag a task up or down and let go, or press Show me. Keyboard: Tab to a grip, Space, arrows, Space.

  • Reply to Sam
  • Book the flights
  • Review the draft
  • Water the plants

Press Space to pick up the task, the up and down arrow keys to move it, and Space again to drop it. Escape puts it back.

2

Try it

Change a setting, then try it again or press Show me.

Slide speed

How fast the other tasks slide out of the way.

How much it lifts

Strong makes the task you hold grow, tilt and cast a deeper shadow.

Only the grip starts a drag, so a finger on the rest of a row still scrolls the page.

3

Copy the prompt

Paste it into an AI assistant such as ChatGPT, Claude or Cursor to build this in your own project.

Add drag to reorder to [the list whose order people can change]. Lift the picked-up item above the list and let it follow the pointer exactly; as it passes the middle of a neighbor, slide that neighbor into the gap, and on release settle it into its new place and save the order. Shift the items rather than changing the layout, so it stays smooth. Support the keyboard: Space picks up, the arrow keys move, Space drops and Escape cancels, each step read out to screen readers. On touch screens, start a drag at once from a grip but only after a short press elsewhere, so the page still scrolls. If the visitor has reduced motion turned on, move items straight to their places.
