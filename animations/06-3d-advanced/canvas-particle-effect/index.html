Canvas Particle Effect

500 particles drifting with O(n²) proximity connections — mouse repels or attracts with configurable force.

— fps

Canvas is right for 50+ particles — DOM bogs down at that scale. O(n²) distance checks are fine below 500 particles; above that, use spatial partitioning (quadtree).
