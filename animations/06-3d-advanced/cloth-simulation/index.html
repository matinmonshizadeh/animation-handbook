Cloth / Soft-Body Simulation

A cloth sways in the wind, and you can drag it around. Best for playful pages.

Copy the prompt

Paste it into an AI assistant such as ChatGPT, Claude or Cursor to build this in your own project.

Add a cloth simulation to [the flag, banner or fabric you want to show]. Model the cloth as a grid of points joined to their neighbours by links of fixed length. In fixed 1/60 s steps, as many as the time since the last frame needs, move every point by its own momentum plus gravity and wind, then pull linked points back toward their proper distance a few times over, so the cloth moves at the same speed on every screen. Pin some points so the cloth hangs, and let the visitor grab and drag the nearest point with the pointer or a finger. Use a coarser grid on phones. If the visitor has reduced motion turned on, show the cloth hanging still. Match the settings listed below.
