Image Distortion on Hover

Fragment shader displaces UV coordinates around the cursor — ripple, push, liquid, or pixelate.

— fps

The shader samples the pattern at distorted UVs — the texture itself never changes. Production: replace the procedural pattern with a texture sampler for real images.
