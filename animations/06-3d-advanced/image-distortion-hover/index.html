Image Distortion on Hover

The picture ripples and bends around your pointer. Best for portfolio images.

1

Hover it

Move your pointer over the picture, drag a finger across it (or Tab to it and use the arrow keys), or press Show me.

Ring: how far the bend reaches Arrows (drawn longer): each spot takes its color from the tip Thin dashes: where the grid lines really are Untouched Bends

Inside, the picture never changes: near the pointer, each spot copies its color from where its arrow points, so the grid bends. The dashed ring is how far the bend reaches.

Untouched ignores the pointer, shown as a ghost. Bends copies each nearby spot's color from a shifted place, so the picture ripples there; point at it, or press Show me.

Copy the prompt

Paste it into an AI assistant such as ChatGPT, Claude or Cursor to build this in your own project.

Add a hover distortion to [the image you want to react]. Draw the image with a WebGL shader and, in a circle around the pointer, read each pixel from a slightly shifted spot, so the picture seems to ripple, bulge, swirl like liquid or break into squares there while the image itself never changes. The bending fades out smoothly after the pointer leaves. On touch screens, dragging a finger across the image should do the same. If the visitor has reduced motion turned on, keep the image still.
