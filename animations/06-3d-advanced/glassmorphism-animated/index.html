Copy the prompt

Paste it into an AI assistant such as ChatGPT, Claude or Cursor to build this in your own project.

Add frosted glass to [the cards or panels you want to style]. Give each card a see-through tinted background, blur whatever sits behind it, and add a thin light edge. Put colorful shapes that drift slowly behind the cards, with a faint pattern of dots over them, because glass over plain color looks grey and shows no frost. Put a see-through dark layer inside each card under its text so the words stay readable. A card can also breathe, its blur growing and fading, or slowly change its tint. Blurring costs a lot, so keep the glass to small cards and blur the shapes less on phones. If the visitor has reduced motion turned on, keep everything still. Match the settings listed below.
