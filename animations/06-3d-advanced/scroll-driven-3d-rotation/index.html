Add a scroll-driven 3D rotation to [the product or object you want to show]. Pin the object in view while the visitor scrolls through a tall section, and turn the scroll position, from the top of the section to its end, into a series of poses: turned to one side, tilted forward, larger, then turned round to show its back. Blend smoothly between neighbouring poses so scrolling back plays it in reverse, and when the settings include it, let the object glide to catch up instead of jumping with the scroll. If the visitor has reduced motion turned on, follow the scroll exactly, with no gliding. Match the settings listed below.
