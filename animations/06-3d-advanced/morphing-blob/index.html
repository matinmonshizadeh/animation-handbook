Morphing Blob

An organic gooey blob built from metaball circles fused by an SVG blur-plus-contrast filter — it drifts on its own and a droplet chases your pointer, merging and splitting like liquid.

Move your pointer

No shaders and no libraries — the whole liquid effect is one SVG filter over plain moving circles. Blur softens, the color-matrix re-hardens the edge, and the metaballs appear to melt together.
