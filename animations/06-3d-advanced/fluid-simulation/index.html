WebGL metaballs — SDF spheres that merge smoothly via smooth-minimum in a GLSL fragment shader.

Real fluid simulation solves Navier-Stokes equations. This is a shortcut: SDF metaballs rendered per-pixel in a fragment shader. They look fluid but are just smoothly merged SDFs.
