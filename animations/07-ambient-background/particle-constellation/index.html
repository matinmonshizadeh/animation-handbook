Particle Constellation

Drifting nodes link to nearby neighbors — line opacity fades with distance, building a living network mesh.

— fps

The whole effect is one rule: for every pair of nodes closer than the link distance, draw a line whose opacity is proportional to how close they are. Nodes never touch — the connections do the work.
