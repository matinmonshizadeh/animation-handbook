07.03 · Ambient & Background

Aurora / Northern Lights

Blurred bands of light sway like the northern lights. Best for dark backgrounds.

1

Watch it

It moves by itself. Pause it to look closely, or turn on slow motion to see each part of the movement.

Aurora
Charged particles. Magnetic fields. Light.
Green is oxygen high in the sky; red is rarer, and higher still.

Inside, shown at half size: each band is a wide, blurred strip of color, wider than the box so its ends never show. Each sways and leans on its own slow cycle.

Too narrow shows bands only as wide as the box, so their soft ends show and leave dark gaps at the sides. Wide enough, as in Normal, keeps the ends out of sight.

Copy the prompt

Paste it into an AI assistant such as ChatGPT, Claude or Cursor to build this in your own project.

Add an aurora background to [the dark section you want it behind]. Stack a few tall, wide bands of color that fade to transparent at the top and bottom, blur them softly, and let each one sway sideways and lean a little on its own slow cycle, so they drift apart and together like curtains of light. Make the bands wider than the section and clip them, so their ends never show. Blur less on phones to keep it smooth. If the visitor has reduced motion turned on, keep the bands still.
