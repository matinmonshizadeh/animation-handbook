Aurora / Northern Lights

Tall color bands drift and skew horizontally — blurred gradient layers faking the waving curtain of light.

Aurora Borealis
Charged particles. Magnetic fields. Light.
Green oxygen at 100km altitude, blue nitrogen below, rare red oxygen above 200km.

Aurora colors are chemistry: green = oxygen at ~100km, red = oxygen above 200km, blue/purple = nitrogen. The visual trick is tall, drifting, blurred vertical bands.
