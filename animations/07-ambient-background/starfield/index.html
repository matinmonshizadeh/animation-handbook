Starfield / Space Particles

Stars stream toward you out of the dark. Best for space themes.

1

Watch it

It moves by itself. Pause it to look closely, or turn on slow motion to see each part of the movement.

Every star flies straight out from the middle

Far: small, dim, slow

Near: big, bright, fast

Out of the box, it starts again in the middle

Farther out: bigger, brighter, faster

Out at the left, back in at the right

Without depth

With depth

Starfield
Infinite depth from a single point
Small, slow stars in the middle grow and speed up toward the edge.

Inside, each line is a star's straight route out from the middle, and its arrow its speed: near the middle stars are small, dim and slow, near the edge big, bright and fast.

Without depth gives every star the same size, brightness and speed, so the field looks flat. With depth makes them grow, brighten and speed up toward you, so it looks deep.

Copy the prompt

Paste it into an AI assistant such as ChatGPT, Claude or Cursor to build this in your own project.

Add a starfield background to [the dark section you want it behind]. Draw many small stars on a canvas and move them out from the center, or sideways past the viewer, so it feels like flying through space. Tie each star's size, brightness and speed to its distance from the center: small, dim and slow near the middle, bigger, brighter and faster toward the edge, which is what creates the depth. A slow speed keeps it calm. Draw fewer stars on phones. If the visitor has reduced motion turned on, show the stars still. Match the settings listed below.
