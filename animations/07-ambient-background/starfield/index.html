Starfield
Infinite depth from a single point
Stars start small at center, grow as they reach the edge — perspective through motion.

Depth illusion: couple size and speed to distance from center. Stars near center = slow, tiny. Stars at edge = fast, large. This single trick creates convincing 3D perspective.
