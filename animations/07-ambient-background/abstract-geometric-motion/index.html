Abstract Geometric Motion

Shapes turn, spread and flow in a calm, endless pattern. Best for music players.

1

Watch it

It moves by itself. Pause it to look closely, or turn on slow motion to see each part of the movement.

Each shape turns at its own pace

The longer the arrow, the faster

Starts over

Never restarts

Inside, each arrow shows which way a shape turns and how fast: every shape keeps its own pace, slowest in the middle and fastest outside.

Starts over jumps back to its first picture every few seconds, as its clock restarts. Never restarts runs on one clock that only grows, so it flows on.
