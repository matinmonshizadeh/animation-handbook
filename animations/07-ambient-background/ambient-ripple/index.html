Ambient Ripple
Something is alive here
Like water drops on a still pond, ECG monitors, sonar pings — suggests presence without interaction.

Ambient ripples evoke "something alive" without requiring interaction. Keep timing IRREGULAR — perfectly periodic ripples feel mechanical, like a clock, not nature.
