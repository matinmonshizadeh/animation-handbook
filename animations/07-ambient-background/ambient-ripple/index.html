Ambient Ripple Effect

Rings spread from a few spots, like drops on a still pond. Best for hero areas.

1

Watch it

It moves by itself. Pause it to look closely, or turn on slow motion to see each part of the movement.

Ambient ripple
Something is alive here
Like drops on a still pond, or a sonar ping: it hints that something is there.

Each spot sends a ring when its circle fills

A ring grows and fades out by the dashed line

Each wait is a little different

Like a clock

Out of step

Inside, each spot's small circle fills up, and when it is full the spot sends a ring. Each ring grows and fades out by the dashed line.

Like a clock sends every ring at the same moment on a steady beat, which feels mechanical. Out of step lets each spot keep its own, uneven time; watch a few seconds.

Add an ambient ripple background to [the section or status you want to feel alive]. Pick a few spots and let each one send out a ring every so often that grows from nothing and gets fainter as it spreads until it disappears, so several rings overlap like drops on a still pond. It runs by itself, never on clicks. When the settings ask for uneven timing, vary the gaps slightly, because perfectly regular rings feel like a clock. If the visitor has reduced motion turned on, show a few still rings. Match the settings listed below.
