Topographic Lines

Lines like those on a hiking map slowly bend and drift. Best for outdoor brands.

1

Watch it

It moves by itself. Pause it to look closely, or turn on slow motion to see each part of the movement.

Hidden hills: lighter is higher

A line at every step of height

Zoomed in: a line runs wherever higher and lower dots meet

Big hills drift Small bumps drift
Hills only
Hills and bumps

Inside, the shading shows the hidden hills, lighter where higher, and each line joins one height, as on a hiking map. Big hills and small bumps drift different ways, so the lines bend.

Hills only slides past in one piece, like a moving picture. Hills and bumps adds small bumps drifting the other way, so over a few seconds its hills swell, sink and change shape.

Copy the prompt

Paste it into an AI assistant such as ChatGPT, Claude or Cursor to build this in your own project.

Add a topographic lines background to [the section you want it behind]. Build a smooth, hilly height map from layered noise and draw its contour lines on a canvas: sample the height on a grid, and in each grid square join the points where the height crosses each level, so every line follows one height, as on a hiking map. Make every fifth line bolder when the settings include it. Shift the noise slowly by elapsed time so the hills swell, shrink and drift, and redraw each frame. Sample a coarser grid on phones.
