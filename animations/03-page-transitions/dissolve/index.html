03.11 · Page Transitions

Dissolve Transition

The page breaks into tiles that give way to the next. Best for photo galleries.

1

Click it

Click Work or About at the top of the box, or press Show me.

Dissolves in tiles

Lighter tiles cover firstCovered in about half a secondThe page swaps once every tile is inThen the tiles vanish at once

Inside, each tile's shade is its turn: lighter tiles cover the page first, and once all are in, the page swaps underneath. Click Work or About, or press Show me.

Plain fade blends the two pages evenly; Dissolves in tiles covers the old page tile by tile in the chosen pattern, then shows the new one. Click Work or About, or press Show me.

2

Change a setting, then try it again or press Show me.

3

Copy the prompt

Paste it into an AI assistant such as ChatGPT, Claude or Cursor to build this in your own project.

Add a dissolve transition to [the pages or images you want to switch between]. Lay a grid of tiles over the old page and fade each tile in at a slightly different moment, following a pattern such as random, a diagonal sweep or rings from the middle, so the old page breaks up instead of fading evenly. Swap the page while the tiles cover it, then remove the tiles, or fade them away in the same pattern to reveal the new page. Give each tile its own delay and let the browser run the fades. If the visitor has reduced motion turned on, use a short, plain fade instead. Match the settings listed below.
