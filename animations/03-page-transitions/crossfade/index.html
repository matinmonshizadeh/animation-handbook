Crossfade Transition

Both pages overlap simultaneously mid-transition — compare with sequential fade-out then fade-in.

True crossfade has both pages visible at ~50% opacity simultaneously. Sequential fade has a brief blank moment. The difference is subtle but crossfade feels smoother.
