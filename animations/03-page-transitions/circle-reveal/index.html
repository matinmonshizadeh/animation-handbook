03.13 · Page Transitions

Circle Reveal

The next page grows in a circle from the spot you click. Best for app-style navigation.

1

Click it

Click anywhere on the page or a page name at the top of the box, or press Show me.

2

Try it

Change a setting, then try it again or press Show me.

Where it starts

Center and Corner (bottom right) ignore where you click.

Speed

How long the circle takes to cover the page.

More optionsFewer options

Going to an earlier page shrinks the old one into the spot instead.

3

Copy the prompt

Paste it into an AI assistant such as ChatGPT, Claude or Cursor to build this in your own project.

Add a circle reveal to [the links or buttons that change the page]. When the visitor clicks or taps, put the new page on top of the old one and show it only inside a circle that starts as a dot and grows until it covers everything. Measure the click at that moment, start the circle there and make its final size reach the farthest corner, so no corner is left uncovered. A press of the keyboard has no spot, so start from the middle of the pressed button. Grow only the visible circle; neither page moves. If the visitor has reduced motion turned on, crossfade the pages instead. Match the settings listed below.
