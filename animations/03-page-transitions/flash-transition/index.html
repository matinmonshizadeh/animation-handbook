Flash / Light Leak Transition

A colored flash peaks at full opacity, hides the page swap, then fades to reveal the new scene.

Flash transitions hide the swap inside an overexposure. When the flash fades, the new content has "always been there". Cinema uses this to hide cuts; web uses it to mask cheap page swaps with style.
