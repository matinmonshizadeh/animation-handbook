Blur Transition

Current page blurs and fades out, then new page sharpens in — a defocus wipe between scenes.

Blur transitions feel cinematic but are GPU-expensive. Modern browsers handle full-page blur fine, but limit it to user-initiated navigation, not autoplay carousels.
