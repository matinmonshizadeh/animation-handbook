Flip In

Swings into view in 3D, like a hinged card turning toward you.

Prompt

Add a 3D flip-in entrance to [the card you want to animate]. The card should start turned edge-on, like a door seen from the side, and swing toward the viewer until it faces them flat. Give the card's container a 3D perspective so the swing has real depth; without it, the flip looks like a flat squash. The hinge can sit in the middle or along one edge, and the card can fade in while it turns. If the visitor has reduced motion turned on, show the card facing forward without the flip. Match the settings listed below.
