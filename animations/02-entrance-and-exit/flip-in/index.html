02.11 · Entrance & Exit

Flip In

Swings into view in 3D, like a card turning over. Best for cards and tiles.

1

Watch it

It plays by itself. Turn on slow motion to see each part of the movement.

⬡
3D Flip
The depth comes from the space around the card. Without it, the turn looks like a flat squash.

From above

Hinge: through the middle

Starts a quarter turn away, see-through

Your eye: at arm's length

Ticks: each tenth of the time; it turns in about half a second

No depth

With depth

A fifth of a second in

Inside, the small drawing shows the card from above: it starts turned away and swings on its hinge to face you. The closer your eye, the deeper the turn looks.

No depth has no 3D space around it, so its turn is a flat squash; With depth swings in real depth. With Loop off, both rest a fifth of a second in.

Try it

Change a setting and the animation plays again.

Swing direction

Sideways turns like a door, up and down like a flap.

Hinge

An edge hinge makes it swing from that side.

Speed

How far it turns

Copy the prompt

Paste it into an AI assistant such as ChatGPT, Claude or Cursor to build this in your own project.

Add a 3D flip-in entrance to [the card you want to animate]. The card should start turned away from the viewer and swing toward them until it faces them flat. Give the card's container a 3D perspective so the swing has real depth; without it, the flip looks like a flat squash. The hinge can sit in the middle or along one edge, and the card can fade in while it turns. If the visitor has reduced motion turned on, show the card facing forward without the flip.
