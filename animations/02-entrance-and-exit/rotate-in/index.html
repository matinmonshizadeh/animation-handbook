Rotate In

Spins while entering — best on radially-symmetric shapes like icons, stars, and gears.

Landed

Rotate-in is best on radially-symmetric shapes — icons, stars, gears, badges. Avoid on text or rectangles. Scale combined with rotation creates an "arriving" feel, not just spinning in place.
