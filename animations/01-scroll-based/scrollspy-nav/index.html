Scrollspy Navigation

A menu highlights the section you are reading. Best for long docs.

1

Scroll it

Scroll inside the box or pick a section in the menu, or press Play and it scrolls for you.

Reading line Lit: the last heading above the line A pressed link glides to its section
Never lights Lights your section

Inside, the dashed line is the reading line: the menu lights the last section whose heading has passed above it. Scroll the box or press Play to watch.

In Never lights the menu stays plain, so you cannot tell where you are; in Lights your section it marks the section you are reading. Scroll the box or press Play to watch.

2

Try it

Change a setting, then scroll again or press Play.

3

Copy the prompt

Paste it into an AI assistant such as ChatGPT, Claude or Cursor to build this in your own project.

Add a scrollspy menu to [your documentation or long page and its section headings]. Keep a menu of section links in place beside the content and highlight the link for the section being read, with a marker that slides to it: a section becomes current once its heading scrolls up past a chosen line, and the last link lights up at the very bottom. Clicking or tapping a link scrolls to its section and lights that link at once, without flickering through the links in between. On phones, turn the menu into a strip across the top. If the visitor has reduced motion turned on, jump to sections instead of gliding. Match the settings listed below.
