Scrollspy Navigation

A nav rail that tracks the section in view — the indicator slides to the active link; clicking a link smooth-scrolls to its section.

Section tops are measured once (container-relative, not offsetTop) and compared against an activation line — scrollTop + 35% of the viewport. The active index only changes when a new section crosses it.
