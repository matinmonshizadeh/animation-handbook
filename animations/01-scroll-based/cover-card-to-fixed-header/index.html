The Architecture
of Scroll Motion

Why a tall cover can fold into a slim bar as you read

Matin M. · May 2026
Matin M.

One number drives it all

As you scroll, one number moves from 0 to 1. The cover's height, the size of the title, the fading background and the small author badge all follow that number, so every moment in between looks planned.

A header should get out of the way without disappearing.

Everything moves together

The title shrinks toward the top-left corner while the subtitle and the date fade out first, so the slim bar never looks crowded.

The author's name fades into the corner of the bar in the second half of the fold, when there is room for it.

Room to read

Once the cover has folded away, only a slim bar stays at the top, with the title and the author, and the rest of the box goes to the text.

Scroll back up and the cover opens again, step by step, in exactly the reverse order.

The end · scroll back up and the cover opens again

Inside, the bars show where along the scroll each part changes, and the line marks where you are: one scroll position sets them all. Scroll the box or press Play to watch.

Scrolls away loses the title once the cover leaves; Shrinks and stays folds it into a slim header that keeps the title in view. Scroll the box or press Play to watch.

Copy the prompt

Paste it into an AI assistant such as ChatGPT, Claude or Cursor to build this in your own project.

Add a collapsing cover to [your article or page header]. Start with a tall cover pinned to the top that holds the title, a subtitle, the date and the author. As the visitor scrolls, shrink it into a slim header: the title scales down into the corner, the subtitle and date fade out first, and a small author badge fades in near the end. Unless the settings make it snap, tie every change to one number that runs from 0 to 1 with the scroll, so every moment in between looks planned and scrolling back reverses it. Turn off scroll anchoring on the scrolling area. If the visitor has reduced motion turned on, switch between the tall and slim cover halfway, without animating. Match the settings listed below.
