Scroll Velocity Skew

Rows shear in proportion to how fast you scroll — velocity, not position, drives the transform, so stopping lets everything spring back straight.

Skew is mapped to smoothed scroll velocity, not scroll position. Scroll fast and the rows shear; the moment you stop, velocity decays to zero and the shear relaxes away.
