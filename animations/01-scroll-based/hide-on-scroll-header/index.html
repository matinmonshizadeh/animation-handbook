Hide-on-Scroll Header

The top bar slides away as you scroll down and comes back as you scroll up. Best for mobile pages.

Field Notes · Design

A bar that steps aside while you read

Scroll down and the bar above slides out of the way. Scroll back up to bring it home.

Scroll down

Reading down the page, you do not need the menu, so the bar slides up out of view and the story gets the whole screen.

Scroll back up

The bar returns: at the first move up, or only once you are back near the top, whichever the page asks for.

Near the top it stays

Close to the top of the page the bar always shows, whichever way you scroll.

Small moves do not count

A few pixels of jitter never flip the bar in and out, so it does not flicker.

It floats over the page

The bar sits on top of the page and moves with a transform, so nothing below it jumps.

Add a hide-on-scroll header to [the top bar of your site]. When the visitor scrolls down, slide the bar up out of view, or shrink it to a slim strip if the settings ask for that; when they scroll back up, bring it back as the settings say. Tell the direction by comparing each scroll position with the last one, ignore moves of a few pixels so the bar never flickers, and always show it near the top of the page. Let the bar float over the content, move it only with a transform and read the scroll once per frame. Touch scrolling counts too. If the visitor has reduced motion turned on, fade the bar out and in instead. Match the settings listed below.
