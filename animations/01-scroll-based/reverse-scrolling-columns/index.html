Reverse-Scrolling Columns

A center column that scrolls normally while two flanking columns scroll in the opposite direction.

The illusion of the side columns 'flowing the other way' is purely visual — they scroll at the same speed as your input, just in the opposite direction. Setting the multiplier to +1.0× makes them move with you and the effect disappears.
