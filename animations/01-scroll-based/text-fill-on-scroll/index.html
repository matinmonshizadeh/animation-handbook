Text Fill on Scroll

A pinned paragraph fills word by word as you scroll — the reading-highlight effect, perfectly reversible.

scroll to read

Scroll progress sweeps an index through the words: everything behind it is lit, everything ahead stays dim. Because the fill is computed from position — not played — scrolling back un-reads the paragraph exactly.
