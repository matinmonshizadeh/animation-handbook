Parallax Scrolling

Elements move at different speeds — background slower, foreground faster — creating a 3D depth illusion.

Real cameras render depth geometrically — layers slow down roughly as 1/distance. Try ratios like 100% / 60% / 30% / 10% for the most believable depth.
