Parallax Depth-of-Field

Five mountain layers translate at different speeds; a focal plane blurs each by its depth distance.

Every layer sits at a fixed depth. The focal plane is one 0–1 value, and a layer's blur is simply its distance from that plane — so racking focus from the far sky to the near ridge is a single number changing.
