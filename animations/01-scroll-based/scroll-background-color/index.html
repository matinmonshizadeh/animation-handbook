Scroll-Driven Background Color

The page background continuously blends between each section's color as you scroll — interpolation through a palette, not a snap at boundaries.

Scroll position maps to a segment of the palette, and the two neighboring colors are interpolated. Toggle "Snap at boundaries" to see the difference between hard cuts and blending — the blend is what sells the effect.
