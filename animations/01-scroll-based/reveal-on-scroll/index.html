01.12 · Scroll-Based

Reveal on Scroll

Cards appear as they cross a line in the box. Best for long landing pages.

1

Scroll it

Scroll inside the box, or press Play. Back to top hides the cards again.

trigger
At or above the line: shown Dashed: where it settles Always shown Shown once
Below: hidden until it crosses

Inside, each card below the line waits faintly in its hidden start; past the line it settles on its dashed outline and stays. Press Play to watch, or press Back to top and scroll.

Always shown has every card in place from the start; Shown once keeps each card hidden until it rises past the dashed line. Press Play to watch, or press Back to top and scroll.

2

Try it

Change a setting, then scroll again or press Play.

Trigger line

How far up the box a card must come to appear.

Cards hide again below the line and replay each time.

3

Copy the prompt

Paste it into an AI assistant such as ChatGPT, Claude or Cursor to build this in your own project.

Add reveal-on-scroll animations to [the sections or cards on your page]. Keep each element in a hidden starting state, such as faded, shifted, shrunk, blurred or clipped, until it rises past a trigger line inside the view, then let it settle into its normal state. Watch the elements with the browser's intersection observer rather than on every scroll, and count an element that is already above the line as crossed, so a fast scroll never skips one. Reveal each element once, or every time it crosses, as the settings say. If the visitor has reduced motion turned on, show each element without movement. Match the settings listed below.
