Highlighter Sweep

A marker sweeps in behind the key words, one after another. Best for key points.

1

Watch it

It plays by itself. Turn on slow motion to see each part of the movement.

Readers skim first. A marker sweeps behind the words that matter, one phrase at a time, so the main idea stands out at a glance.

Each word has its own band, drawn behind it One phrase in about two thirds of a second, with a short pause between Shown part way through Marked at once
Swept in orderPart way through

Inside, every key word has its own band behind it, so a phrase that wraps is marked line by line. The phrases are swept in order, 1, 2, 3, with a short pause between.

Marked at once puts every band down in one go. Swept in order draws each phrase like a pen, one after another, so the eye follows the key words.

Copy the prompt

Paste it into an AI assistant such as ChatGPT, Claude or Cursor to build this in your own project.

Add a highlighter sweep to [the key words in your paragraph or quote]. Behind each key phrase, a band of marker color grows from left to right, as if drawn with a highlighter pen, and the phrases are marked one after another in reading order. Draw the band behind the text, never over it, and grow it by scaling it from its left edge, so the words never move or reflow. When a phrase wraps onto a second line, sweep each line in turn, as one continuous stroke. Keep the words dark enough to read on the marker color. If the visitor has reduced motion turned on, fade the highlights in instead of sweeping them. Match the settings listed below.
