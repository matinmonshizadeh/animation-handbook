Split-Flap Board

Tiles flip through the letters to spell a word, like an airport board. Best for hero titles.

1

Watch it

It plays by itself. Turn on slow motion to see each part of the movement.

Departures

The old letter's top falls forward on the hinge

then the next letter's bottom swings down

This tile flips 18 times, from A up to R, about 12 flips a second

Shown part way through a flip

Appears at once

Flips

Part way through

Inside, each tile is two still halves and two flaps on a hinge. Flip after flip, the old letter's top falls and the next one's bottom lands, until the tile reaches its letter.

Appears at once puts the word on the board in one go. Flips turns each tile through the alphabet, so the word settles tile by tile, like a real departures board.

Copy the prompt

Paste it into an AI assistant such as ChatGPT, Claude or Cursor to build this in your own project.

Add a split-flap board to [your headline or short word]. Show each letter on its own tile, split across the middle like an old airport departures board. To change a tile, flip it through the alphabet one letter at a time until it reaches its letter: the top half of the old letter falls forward over the hinge, turning in 3D and darkening as it turns, and the bottom half of the next letter swings down into place. Start the tiles a moment apart from left to right, so they settle one by one. Turn the halves with transforms only, and let screen readers hear the final word once. If the visitor has reduced motion turned on, fade the letters in instead. Match the settings listed below.
