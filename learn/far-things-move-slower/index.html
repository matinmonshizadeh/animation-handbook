Lesson 21 of 25 · Idea

Far things move slower

Look out of a moving train: the fence rushes by, the hills crawl and the moon seems to stay put. Your eyes use that difference to judge distance.

1

Watch it

A view from a train window. Each layer slides past at its own speed.

  • Moon
  • Mountains
  • Hills
  • Fence
2

Try it

Give every layer the same speed and watch the depth disappear.

On, the scene slides like one flat picture. Off, near things pass faster and it looks deep.

Why it works

Near things cross your view quickly and far things slowly, so your eyes read the difference as distance. Give a picture's far layers small moves and its near layers big moves, and a flat picture looks deep. That is the whole idea behind parallax, and it works best with a few layers and gentle speeds.

Where you'll see it

Parallax ScrollingFar layers move slower than near ones as you scroll. Parallax Depth-of-FieldLayers move at their own speed as the focus shifts. 2.5D / Pseudo-3DFlat layers slide by different amounts, faking depth. Depth-Map PhotoA flat photo turns 3D as you point: near parts shift more. Wave LayersLayers of waves drift sideways, each at its own speed.
