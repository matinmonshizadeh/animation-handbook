Learning path

Learn web animation

25 short lessons, from easy to advanced. You watch and try; you never write code.

Chapter 1Appear and leave

  1. 1Idea · Not built yetHow long it takesFast feels snappy and slow feels calm: pick the time that fits the moment.
  2. 2AnimationFade In / Fade OutFades in to appear and fades out to leave.
  3. 3Idea · Not built yetSlowing downReal things speed up and slow down, so smooth animations do too.
  4. 4AnimationSlide InTravels into place from one edge.
  5. 5AnimationScale In / Zoom InGrows from smaller to full size.

Chapter 2Answer the visitor

  1. 6Idea · Not built yetAnswer every actionA press, a tap or a hover gets a quick, small reply, so people know it worked.
  2. 7AnimationButton Press ScaleShrinks as you press it and springs back as you let go.
  3. 8AnimationHover State AnimationSix ways a card can react when you point at it.
  4. 9AnimationToggle / Switch SlideThe knob slides across as the switch turns on or off.
  5. 10AnimationLoading SpinnerSix small shapes loop to show that something is loading.

Chapter 3Move with the scroll

  1. 11Idea · Not built yetFollows the scrollSome animations play by themselves; others move only when you scroll.
  2. 12AnimationReveal on ScrollCards appear as they cross a line in the box.
  3. 13Idea · Not built yetOne after anotherA short wait between items leads the eye through a group.
  4. 14AnimationStagger RevealItems in a group appear one after another as it scrolls in.
  5. 15AnimationProgress BarA bar fills as you read down the page.

Chapter 4Change pages

  1. 16Idea · Not built yetKeep your placeWhen the page changes, motion shows where things went.
  2. 17AnimationCrossfade TransitionThe old page fades out as the new one fades in.
  3. 18AnimationSlide TransitionPages slide across, and going back slides the other way.
  4. 19AnimationShared Element TransitionA picture grows from the list into the next page's header.
  5. 20AnimationFLIP TechniqueCards glide to their new places when the layout changes.

Chapter 5Depth and 3D

  1. 21Idea · Not built yetFar things move slowerNear things pass quickly and far things slowly: that is how depth looks.
  2. 22AnimationParallax ScrollingFar layers move slower than near ones as you scroll.
  3. 23AnimationParallax 3D TiltA card tilts toward your pointer, and a light slides over it.
  4. 24Animation3D Flip CardA card turns over in 3D to show its back.
  5. 25Idea · Not built yetLess is moreWhen not to animate, and how to respect people who ask for less motion.
