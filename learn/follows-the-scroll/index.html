Follows the scroll

Some animations play on their own, like a video. Others follow your scroll: they move when you scroll and stop when you stop.

1

Scroll it

Scroll the right box, or press Play and it scrolls for you. The left ball keeps its own time.

Plays by itself

Moves on a timer, whatever you do.

Follows the scroll

Goes where the scroll goes. 0% scrolled

Why it works

When an animation follows the scroll, the visitor controls it: scroll slowly and it moves slowly, scroll back and it plays backward, stop and it stops. That ties the movement to reading the page, so it feels like part of it. An animation that plays by itself runs on a clock instead, whatever the visitor does.
