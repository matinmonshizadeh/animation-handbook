Field notes

A slow week by the water

The ferry leaves at seven and comes back at dusk. In between, the harbor belongs to gulls, nets and the people who mend them.

Most days begin the same way: a cup of tea, a walk along the pier and a long look at the sky, to guess what the afternoon will bring.

Mid-morning

Down the lane, the bakery opens its shutters. The smell of warm bread reaches the corner long before the door does.

A boy delivers the newspapers on a bicycle that is a little too big for him. He knows every house by its gate.

By noon the benches fill with neighbors, and the talk drifts from weather to tides to whose turn it is to host dinner.
